Research

My research is on deep learning for time series and sequential data: how sequence models such as LSTMs, GRUs, temporal convolutional networks and attention-based hybrids learn from data that unfolds over time, how to make their predictions more accurate, and how they behave when that data changes.

I have worked on hybrid ensembles for forecasting and load prediction, and on sequence models trained on hospital time series that have to cope with years of shifting data. As a freelance AI engineer, I also build production AI systems: real-time voice agents, retrieval-augmented LLM applications and computer-vision pipelines.

Time series forecastingHybrid deep learning ensembles (BiLSTM, BiGRU, TCN, attention, XGBoost) for forecasting and load prediction.
Sequence models under changeHow recurrent and tree-based models behave, and how to update them, when sequential data drifts over time.
Explaining sequence modelsWhether feature attributions (Integrated Gradients, SHAP) stay stable when a model is updated.

Publications and preprints

MIDI 2026Towards Reliable ML Evaluations: A Temporal-Validity Audit for Trustworthy Model Updates

Fatema Ferdous Tamanna, K. M. Merajul Arefin, Md. Abdul Masud

A temporal-validity audit that checks, before a model update is evaluated, whether apparent shift in MIMIC-IV ICU time series (2008–2022) reflects real change or the database recording process. Re-scoring the same models on audited data reversed one update comparison from +0.051 to −0.030 AUROC. Springer LNCS. Accepted, to appear.

PreprintAudit Before You Update: Recording Artifacts and the Limits of Bounded Model Updates in MIMIC-IV ICU Prediction

Sequence models (LSTM and gradient-boosted trees) on ICU time series from 2008–2022: audit recording artifacts before any update, detect drift without labels, and compare bounded against full model updates with pre-specified non-inferiority margins.

Under reviewBeyond the Update Boundary: Explanation Reproducibility in Clinical Time-Series Models

Fatema Ferdous Tamanna, K. M. Merajul Arefin, Md. Abdul Masud

Measures how reproducible a time-series model's explanations are after it is updated: a two-stream model (LSTM over hourly ICU physiology, MLP over treatment context) updated across three eras with its encoder frozen or fine-tuned, at four learning rates and five seeds.
